# House style (from the harness's original modules)

- Module starts with a one-line docstring describing what it is for.
- Functions have a one-line docstring in the imperative or descriptive voice.
- snake_case names, short and plain (text, rows, total). No abbreviations like ch or buf.
- Prefer comprehensions and small helpers over long loops with flags.
- No comments that restate the code; no license header lines in module files.
